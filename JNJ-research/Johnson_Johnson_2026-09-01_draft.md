## Decision and user

**Decision:** Whether to initiate public-equity research coverage and, after completing valuation work, potentially initiate an investment in Johnson & Johnson common stock (NYSE: JNJ).

**User:** A long-only public-equity investor who needs an evidence-based initial view, a transparent enterprise-to-equity bridge, and a research plan before committing capital.

## Company and valuation date

**Entity:** Johnson & Johnson, a New Jersey corporation (CIK 0000200406; EIN 22-1024240).

**Valuation / information date:** December 28, 2025, the balance-sheet and fiscal-year-end date in the primary filing. The filing was submitted February 11, 2026. No later information is used as valuation evidence in this Edition A.

## Initial thesis

**Watch / defer.** JNJ has substantial cash generation and strong oncology growth, particularly DARZALEX, but the investment case needs explicit work on the durability of that growth, offsetting STELARA biosimilar erosion, acquisition returns, litigation exposure, and the value implied by the market price. This is not yet an initiate recommendation.

## Consequential assumptions

- Cash and marketable securities are available to equity holders at carrying value; this needs validation for trapped cash, operating cash needs, and any class-specific convention.
- Loans and notes payable plus long-term debt are the debt claims used in the first-pass bridge. The bridge excludes lease liabilities and other non-debt obligations unless the class method says otherwise.
- Weighted-average diluted shares are an appropriate first-pass denominator for the synthetic bridge. A point-in-time diluted share count may be more appropriate if the class requires it.
- DARZALEX growth and other oncology growth can partly offset the earnings and cash-flow effects of STELARA biosimilar erosion; this is an assumption to test, not a conclusion.

## Unknowns

- The synthetic enterprise value and the instructor’s exact bridge convention / known answer.
- The valuation-date share price, implied market capitalization, and market-implied growth / margin assumptions.
- Product-level durability, pricing, patent / regulatory timelines, and the economics of the Intra-Cellular acquisition.
- The magnitude, timing, and probability-weighted value of litigation and contingent liabilities.
- Whether cash, marketable securities, leases, pension items, or contingent consideration require adjustment under the course convention.

## Research plan

1. Insert the class synthetic EV and calculate the bridge mechanically; reconcile it to the known answer before changing any assumptions.
2. Establish the authorized valuation-date share price and convert it to equity value using the course-specified diluted-share convention.
3. Build a product-level revenue model for DARZALEX, STELARA, TREMFYA, CARVYKTI, SPRAVATO, and major MedTech franchises; separately model price, volume, foreign exchange, loss of exclusivity, and biosimilar effects.
4. Normalize earnings and free cash flow for acquisition, integration, restructuring, litigation, and other non-recurring or non-operating items; assess acquisition returns and leverage capacity.
5. Review the filing’s risk factors, legal-proceedings note, debt maturities, and cash accessibility; triangulate key product assumptions against primary clinical, regulatory, and competitor sources.
6. Value JNJ with a DCF and comparable-company cross-check; run downside cases centered on faster biosimilar erosion, weaker oncology uptake, pricing pressure, and higher legal costs. Initiate only if upside compensates for these risks.
