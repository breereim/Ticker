# Johnson & Johnson (NYSE: JNJ): Research Update

**Information date:** September 3, 2026  
**Purpose:** FIN 43900 learning exercise; supplemental analysis to [the September 1 Edition A memo](Johnson_Johnson_2026-09-01_Project1_Edition_A.md).  
**Source register:** [sources.md](sources.md)

## Thesis in one paragraph

Johnson & Johnson is a high-quality healthcare franchise with a growing Innovative Medicine business, strong oncology products, and material operating cash generation; its FY2025 sales were $94.2bn, up 6.0%, and its Q2 2026 sales were $25.31bn, up 6.6%. [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm) [Q2 2026 results](https://www.jnj.com/media-center/press-releases/johnson-johnson-reports-q2-2026-results-raises-2026-outlook) The counterweights are STELARA biosimilar erosion, negative FY2025 price contribution, acquisition-funded leverage, and legal uncertainty around talc. [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm) [AP News, July 28, 2026](https://apnews.com/article/abecc65008f04bf25a9c7fcbb7514be7) The current evidence supports further diligence, not a valuation-based purchase decision, because this report does not establish an intrinsic-value range against a current market price.

## What changed across the annual filings

| Fiscal year | Reported sales | MD&A evidence and interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| 2023 | $85.2bn | The company had two operating segments—then called Pharmaceutical and MedTech—and reported that it had completed the Kenvue separation in 2023; historical comparisons should therefore be read as a post-consumer-healthcare portfolio transition. [FY2023 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040624000013/jnj-20231231.htm) |
| 2024 | $88.8bn | Worldwide sales rose 4.3%; management identified volume growth of 5.9%, flat price, and a 1.6% currency drag. [FY2024 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040625000038/jnj-20241229.htm) |
| 2025 | $94.2bn | Worldwide sales rose 6.0%; volume added 8.4%, price subtracted 3.1%, and currency added 0.7%. Acquisitions and divestitures added 1.1 percentage points, principally CAPLYTA and Shockwave. [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm) |

The three filings show a larger post-Kenvue company and consistently positive volume contribution, but the 2025 composition is less simple than the headline: acquired revenue and volume growth offset negative pricing and a large loss of STELARA sales. [FY2023 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040624000013/jnj-20231231.htm) [FY2024 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040625000038/jnj-20241229.htm) [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm) My inference is that a forecast should separate legacy volume, net price, currency, acquisitions, and loss-of-exclusivity effects instead of extrapolating a single sales-growth percentage from the historical series. [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm)

## Product engine versus the key offset

Innovative Medicine generated $60.4bn in FY2025 sales, up 6.0% reported and 5.3% operationally; the segment’s STELARA decline reduced operational growth by approximately 10.4%, primarily because of biosimilar competition. [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm) Oncology sales were $25.38bn, including DARZALEX at $14.351bn (+23.0%), CARVYKTI at $1.887bn (+95.9%), and ERLEADA at $3.574bn (+19.2%); IMBRUVICA declined 7.1% to $2.823bn. [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm)

This is encouraging product breadth, but it does not prove that the growth can fully replace STELARA over time. The investment model should use product-specific erosion and adoption scenarios, especially for DARZALEX, CARVYKTI, TREMFYA, CAPLYTA, and the MedTech franchises, rather than assume the oncology growth rate persists. This is an analytical judgment based on the product concentration and offsetting trends disclosed in the FY2025 MD&A. [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm)

## Profit and cash-flow quality

FY2025 consolidated earnings before income taxes were $32.6bn (34.6% of sales), compared with $16.7bn (18.8%) in FY2024. [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm) The comparison is not a clean measure of recurring margin expansion: “Other (income) expense, net” included approximately $7.0bn from reversing a significant portion of a previously accrued talc reserve, partly offset by a $0.8bn Auris shareholder-litigation expense. [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm)

Cash flow from operations was $24.5bn in FY2025, with $4.8bn of PP&E additions; the simple operating-cash-flow-less-PP&E calculation is therefore about $19.7bn before acquisitions and other investing uses. [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm) That is a useful cash-generation indicator, not reported free cash flow and not cash available solely for shareholders, because J&J also spent $17.5bn on acquisitions, $12.4bn on dividends, and $6.0bn on share repurchases in FY2025. [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm)

My normalized-earnings approach would exclude or separately scenario-model the talc-reserve reversal, litigation expense, acquisition / integration items, acquired in-process R&D, and intangible amortization. That treatment is a valuation judgment, not an assertion that these costs are irrelevant; acquisitions and legal obligations can be recurring economic demands even when accounting presentation changes. The underlying disclosures are in the [FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm).

## Acquisition financing and balance-sheet risk

At the end of FY2025, cash and equivalents were $19.7bn, marketable securities were about $0.4bn, total debt was $47.9bn, and management reported net debt of $27.8bn, compared with $12.1bn in FY2024. [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm) The company issued approximately $9.2bn of senior unsecured notes in the first quarter of 2025; it said proceeds helped fund the approximately $14.5bn Intra-Cellular Therapies acquisition that closed April 2, 2025. [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm)

The balance sheet is still supported by substantial cash generation, but rising net debt makes acquisition-return assumptions more consequential. This is an inference from the disclosed increase in net debt and the use of acquisition spending; it should be tested with debt-maturity, interest-expense, and downside cash-flow scenarios before a buy decision. [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm)

## Recent evidence: management’s call and independent news

On its July 15, 2026 Q2 earnings call, hosted by Chairman and CEO Joaquin Duato and CFO Joseph Wolk, J&J reported Q2 sales of $25.310bn (+6.6% reported; +5.6% operational) and increased its 2026 midpoint reported-sales outlook to $101.1bn and adjusted-EPS outlook to $11.68. [Q2 2026 earnings-call event](https://investor.jnj.com/events-and-presentations/events/event-details/2026/Johnson--Johnson-Second-Quarter-2026-Earnings-Call/) [Q2 2026 results and reconciliations](https://www.jnj.com/media-center/press-releases/johnson-johnson-reports-q2-2026-results-raises-2026-outlook) Management’s increased outlook is evidence of its current expectations, not independent confirmation of future results. [Q2 2026 results and reconciliations](https://www.jnj.com/media-center/press-releases/johnson-johnson-reports-q2-2026-results-raises-2026-outlook)

Recent reporting supplies both support and risk context. Reuters reported that Q2 growth in TREMFYA and DARZALEX more than offset erosion in older products, while noting the quarter’s adjusted EPS beat analyst expectations. [Reuters, July 15, 2026, republished by Fidelity](https://www.fidelity.com/news/article/company-news/202607150620RTRSNEWSCOMBINED_L1N43G18X_1) Separately, AP reported on July 28 that J&J agreed to a proposed $5.5bn resolution of remaining talc-related ovarian-cancer claims, conditioned on participation by at least 95% of remaining claimants, with an initial payment of no more than $3bn next year and no additional payments due until 2028. [AP News, July 28, 2026](https://apnews.com/article/abecc65008f04bf25a9c7fcbb7514be7) Until the condition and final cash timing are resolved, I treat the proposed settlement as a material uncertainty rather than a completed legal outcome. This is an inference from the settlement condition reported by AP. [AP News, July 28, 2026](https://apnews.com/article/abecc65008f04bf25a9c7fcbb7514be7)

## Diligence checklist before a valuation decision

1. Model product-level sales with explicit STELARA erosion, oncology adoption, price / rebate, currency, and acquisition assumptions. [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm)
2. Rebuild a normalized operating-profit and free-cash-flow bridge that does not treat the talc-reserve reversal as recurring operating performance. [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm)
3. Test Intra-Cellular and Shockwave returns against the incremental debt, integration spending, and revenue contribution. [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm)
4. Model legal cash outflows using the condition and payment timing of the proposed talc resolution, including a case where the resolution does not complete. [AP News, July 28, 2026](https://apnews.com/article/abecc65008f04bf25a9c7fcbb7514be7)
5. Establish a current share price and calculate a valuation range before converting the business assessment into an investment recommendation. This is required because the present report contains no market-price comparison.

## Current call

**Watch / defer (wait for more information).** The operating evidence is constructive, especially oncology growth and Q2 guidance, but a buy call would be premature without a normalized valuation, a clearer post-STELARA revenue bridge, and resolution or reliable cash-flow modeling of talc exposure. [FY2025 10-K, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm) [Q2 2026 results](https://www.jnj.com/media-center/press-releases/johnson-johnson-reports-q2-2026-results-raises-2026-outlook) [AP News, July 28, 2026](https://apnews.com/article/abecc65008f04bf25a9c7fcbb7514be7)

I would change this call to initiate if a conservative, product-level valuation showed a sufficient margin of safety after incorporating continued STELARA erosion, acquisition returns, and a credible talc cash-outflow case.
