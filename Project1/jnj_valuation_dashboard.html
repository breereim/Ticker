Comparable-company policy & method disagreement

Policy fixed before selection: listed USD operating companies with global regulated healthcare-product economics, positive FY2025 GAAP diluted EPS, and material medical-product or prescription-medicine exposure. Excludes insurers, providers, distributors, CRO/CMOs, consumer-only companies, and loss-making biotech.

Qualified peers: ABT (diversified medical products; diagnostics/nutritionals differ) and MRK (global pharma/vaccines; lacks MedTech and includes Animal Health). On the same FY2025/same-date basis, peer-median EV/EBIT implies about $206/share; reported-GAAP P/E implies $219–$306/share (median $263).

Why methods disagree: DCF rests on forecast FCFF, WACC and a large terminal value. EV/EBIT reflects peer mix and EBIT definition. P/E is affected by JNJ’s talc-reserve reversal comparability issue. Transaction evidence is excluded: no defensible control premium, synergy or time-consistent comparable transaction is in scope.
