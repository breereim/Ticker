## Initial thesis: Watch / defer

JNJ warrants continued diligence but not an initiate recommendation at this stage. The Company combines substantial operating cash generation with a differentiated Innovative Medicine portfolio and rapid growth in key oncology products. However, the investment case must demonstrate that growth in products such as DARZALEX can sustainably offset STELARA’s biosimilar-driven erosion, while acquisition spending, leverage, litigation, and policy / reimbursement risk remain manageable. A view on intrinsic value also requires the course-prescribed enterprise-to-equity bridge and a valuation-date market-price comparison, neither of which is established here.

## Consequential assumptions

1. Cash and marketable securities can be credited to equity value at carrying value. Further work must determine whether any cash is operationally required, restricted, or otherwise unavailable.
2. The first-pass debt definition is loans and notes payable plus long-term debt. Lease liabilities, pension obligations, contingent consideration, and other claims are excluded unless the course convention requires their inclusion.
3. FY2025 diluted weighted-average shares provide an appropriate first-pass per-share denominator. A point-in-time diluted count may be preferable for a market-date valuation.
4. Growth in DARZALEX and other Innovative Medicine products can offset a meaningful portion of the cash-flow effects of STELARA’s biosimilar erosion. This is a testable underwriting assumption, not a conclusion.
5. Reported operating cash flow is a useful starting point for valuation but requires adjustment for acquisition, integration, litigation, restructuring, tax, and working-capital effects.

## Unknowns requiring resolution

- The synthetic enterprise value, prescribed adjustments, and known answer supplied in class.
- The market price and market capitalization as of the authorized valuation date, together with the resulting market-implied operating assumptions.
- Product-level volume, pricing, loss-of-exclusivity, reimbursement, and competitive trajectories—especially for DARZALEX, STELARA, TREMFYA, CARVYKTI, and SPRAVATO.
- The economic returns and integration risks of recent acquisitions, including Intra-Cellular Therapies.
- The amount, timing, and probability-weighted impact of litigation, policy, and regulatory exposures.
- The appropriate treatment of leases, pension obligations, contingent consideration, and potentially restricted / operating cash in the final bridge.

2. Establish the authorized valuation-date share price and diluted-share convention; calculate market capitalization, enterprise value, and implied valuation multiples.
